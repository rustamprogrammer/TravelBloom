About Us

Small discoveries. Lasting memories.

TravelBloom is a travel recommendation concept that helps curious travelers discover beaches, temples and countries. Our mission is to make exploring new places feel approachable, with clear descriptions and a little inspiration.

We value cultural understanding, thoughtful planning and respect for local communities. Our recommendations are a starting point: check local guidance, opening hours and travel requirements before your trip.

This demonstration website was created for the IBM JavaScript Programming Essentials final project. The team and contact details below are fictional.
